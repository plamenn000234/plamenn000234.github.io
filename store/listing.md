Chefsheet is a recipe library built for professional kitchens: hotels, banquets, buffets, restaurants and catering.

SCALE ANY RECIPE
• 4,900+ technical recipe cards in 100+ collections: cuisines, courses, buffet, banquet, pastry, bakery, fine dining and more
• Set your portions and every component follows, in grams
• Tick off ingredients and steps as you cook

COOK MODE AND TIMERS
• Larger text and the screen stays on
• Tap any time in a method step to start a timer with sound and vibration

ORDER LISTS
• Add dishes, set guests, grams per guest and a popularity factor
• Get a gross (as-bought) order list by section, with trim losses included
• Export to PDF or CSV, or share with your supplier

KNOW WHAT IS IN EVERY DISH
• 14 allergens on every recipe, allergen-free filters
• Vegetarian and vegan filters, calories and nutrition per portion

LEARN
• 10 modules and 49 short lessons: knife skills, food safety, meat and fish, sauces, buffet sizing, food cost
• Tests with PDF certificates
• Kitchen tools: doneness, sous-vide, salt and brines, yield, baker's percentage, buffet planner, food cost, glossary EN · BG · RU

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Favourites, orders and progress stay on your device.

Allergen and nutrition data are derived from ingredients and are estimates. Always check product labels.
