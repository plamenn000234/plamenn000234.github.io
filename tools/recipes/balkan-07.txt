=== Salata od Hobotnice · Dalmatian Octopus Salad · Potato, Capers, Red Onion
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 10 · pg 200 · plates
tag Starters & canapés / Seafood
## OCTOPUS
: Frozen-thawed octopus is more tender · cooked weight ≈ 50 %
Octopus, whole | 2500
Bay leaves | 2
Black peppercorns | 3
Wine vinegar | 50
1. Thaw at 0–4 °C 24 h. Dip three times in boiling water (curls the tentacles), then simmer covered with bay, peppercorns and vinegar 50–70 min until a skewer slides into the thickest part.
2. Cool in the liquor 30 min; chill to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min. Cut tentacles into 1.5 cm pieces.
## SALAD
Octopus, cooked (above) | 1200
Potatoes, waxy | 500
Red onion | 150
Capers | 40
Cherry tomatoes | 250
Olive oil | 150
Red wine vinegar | 40
Parsley | 30
Garlic | 10
Salt | 8
Black pepper | 2
Yield | 2000
1. Boil potatoes in the skin, peel and dice 1.5 cm while warm.
2. Mix octopus, potatoes, sliced onion, capers and halved tomatoes; dress with oil, vinegar, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
3. Marinate 1 h at 0–4 °C; serve cool, not fridge-cold.
st Cooked octopus 0–3 °C 72 h. Dressed salad 0–4 °C 24 h.

=== Gregada · Hvar White Fish & Potato Stew · Olive Oil, Wine, Parsley
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 10 · pg 400 · plates
## GREGADA
: Wide shallow pot · never stirred, only shaken
White fish (scorpion fish, monkfish, conger), cleaned | 2200
Potatoes | 1500
Onion | 300
Garlic | 40
Olive oil | 250
White wine | 300
Water | 500
Parsley | 50
Salt | 20
Black pepper | 4
Yield | 4000
1. Cut fish into 120 g pieces on the bone; salt 15 min.
2. Layer in the pot: half the oil, sliced potatoes (5 mm), onion, garlic, parsley; fish on top; season; pour over wine, water and remaining oil.
3. Bring to a boil, cover; simmer 25–30 min, shaking the pot often, until potatoes are cooked and the liquid has emulsified to a light sauce. Fish core ≥ 63 °C.
## SERVICE
1. Serve in the pot or a deep plate with bread for the sauce.
st Cook to order or within 30 min of service. Leftover: chill ≤ 5 °C within 90 min, 0–3 °C 24 h; reheat once to ≥ 75 °C.

=== Škampi na Buzaru · Kvarner Langoustines in Tomato, Wine & Garlic · Breadcrumbs
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 10 · pg 300 · plates
tag Mains / Seafood
## SCAMPI
: Whole langoustines, cooked to order in batches of 2–3 portions
Langoustines, whole | 3000
Olive oil | 150
Garlic | 50
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 600
White wine | 300
Breadcrumbs | 60
Parsley | 50
Salt | 12
Black pepper | 3
Yield | 3000
1. Rinse langoustines; keep at 0–2 °C on ice.
2. Per batch: warm oil with sliced garlic (no colour); add langoustines, toss 1 min; add wine, reduce 1 min; add crushed tomatoes, salt and pepper.
3. Cover and cook 4–5 min, shaking, until shells are bright and the meat opaque (core ≥ 63 °C).
4. Stir in breadcrumbs to bind the sauce; finish with parsley. Serve with bread and finger bowls.
st Live or chilled langoustines 0–2 °C, use within 24 h. Cook to order.

=== Bakalar na Bijelo · Salt Cod with Potatoes, Garlic & Olive Oil · Christmas Eve
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 10 · pg 250 · plates
tag Starters & canapés / Fish
## COD
: Dried stockfish or salt cod · soak 48–72 h
Salt cod, dried | 800
Water | 3000
1. Soak in cold water at 0–4 °C 48–72 h, changing water twice a day, until it is soft and pleasantly salty.
2. Simmer 30–40 min until it flakes; pick out skin and bones; flake by hand. Keep 300 ml cooking water.
## BAKALAR
Potatoes | 1200
Garlic | 60
Olive oil | 350
Parsley | 50
Black pepper | 4
Salt | 6
Yield | 2500
1. Boil potatoes, peel and crush coarsely.
2. Beat cod and potatoes with crushed garlic, alternating olive oil and warm cooking water, to a light, creamy mass with flakes still visible.
3. Season with pepper and parsley. Serve warm or at room temperature with toasted bread or polenta.
st 0–4 °C 72 h; serve at room temperature, max 2 h out of the fridge.

=== Purica s Mlincima · Zagorje Roast Turkey · Mlinci Flatbread Pasta, Pan Juices
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 10 · pg 420 · plates
## TURKEY
: Brined 12 h
Turkey legs, bone-in | 3500
Water (brine) | 3000
Salt (brine) | 180
Butter | 120
Garlic | 30
Rosemary | 5
Black pepper | 4
1. Brine legs in 6 % brine 12 h at 0–4 °C; rinse and dry.
2. Rub with butter, garlic and pepper; roast 160 °C 2 h, basting, then 200 °C 15 min. Core ≥ 80 °C at the thigh. Rest 15 min; keep the pan juices.
## MLINCI
Wheat flour | 600
Eggs | 100
Water | 250
Salt | 8
Turkey pan juices (above) | 600
Yield | 4200
1. Knead a firm dough; roll very thin; bake on a dry tray 220 °C 5–6 min until crisp and blistered.

## SERVICE
1. 200 g turkey, 200 g mlinci.
st Turkey cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Baked mlinci sheets keep dry 2 weeks.

=== Čobanac · Slavonian Shepherd's Stew · Three Meats, Hot Paprika
at Adriatic & Balkan / Croatia & Dalmatia · N 12 · pg 450 · bowls
## STEW
: Cauldron or 15 L rondeau · 3 h
Beef shin, boneless | 1000
Pork shoulder, boneless | 1000
Wild boar shoulder | 800
Pork fat | 100
Onion | 1500
Garlic | 40
Paprika, sweet | 50
Hot paprika | 10
Tomato paste | 80
Red wine | 500
Water | 2500
Bay leaves | 3
Salt | 30
Black pepper | 5
Yield | 5400
1. Cut meats into 3 cm cubes. Melt fat; sweat onions 20 min until soft and golden.
2. Add meats, sear 10 min; add garlic, paprikas off the heat, paste, wine and bay.
