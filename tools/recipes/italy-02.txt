=== Ricciarelli di Siena · Soft Almond Paste Biscuits · Orange, Icing Sugar
at Italian / Dolci (Desserts) · N 30 · pg 30 · portions (1 piece)
tag Desserts
## PASTE
: Rest the shaped biscuits overnight so the crackled crust forms
Almonds, blanched | 400
Icing sugar | 350
Egg whites | 90
Orange zest | 6
Honey | 20
Vanilla | 2
Almond extract | 1
1. Grind almonds with 150 g icing sugar to a fine flour (do not let it turn oily).
2. Whip whites to soft peaks; fold in almonds, the rest of the sugar except 50 g, zest, honey and flavourings to a firm paste.
3. Shape 30 g lozenges; roll in the remaining icing sugar; place on baking paper; rest uncovered 12 h at room temperature.
## BAKE
Icing sugar | 50
Yield | 900
1. Bake 150 °C 12–14 min: pale, cracked, soft inside. Cool; dust with icing sugar.
st Airtight, room temperature 2 weeks.

=== Torrone Morbido · Soft Honey & Almond Nougat · Hazelnut, Wafer
at Italian / Dolci (Desserts) · N 40 · pg 25 · portions
tag Desserts
## NOUGAT
: Work with a thermometer · the syrup goes to 145 °C
Honey | 250
Sugar | 400
Glucose syrup | 100
Water | 100
Egg whites | 60
Almonds, blanched, toasted | 350
Hazelnuts, toasted | 150
Vanilla | 2
Wafer paper (ostia) | 20
Yield | 1300
1. Heat honey to 121 °C. Meanwhile whip whites to soft peaks; pour in the honey in a thin stream.
2. Cook sugar, glucose and water to 145 °C; pour into the whites with the mixer running; whisk 5 min until thick.
3. Fold in warm nuts and vanilla.
4. Spread between two sheets of wafer paper in a 30 × 20 cm frame, 2.5 cm thick; press; set 12 h. Cut 40 bars.
st Wrapped, dry, room temperature 2 months.
