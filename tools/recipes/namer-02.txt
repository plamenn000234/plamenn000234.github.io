tag Mains / Beef & veal
## BEEF
Beef chuck roast | 3500
Yellow mustard | 40
Coarse sea salt | 30
Black pepper, coarse | 25
Garlic powder | 8
## WRAP
Beef stock | 300
Beef tallow or butter | 60
Yield | 2500
1. Trim hard fat; tie into an even shape; slather with mustard; season.
2. Smoke at 125 °C over oak to core 72 °C and a firm bark (~4 h).
3. Wrap in butcher paper or foil with stock and tallow; cook to core 95 °C, probe-tender (~3 h).
4. Rest in a warm box ≥1 h (above 63 °C). Slice 1 cm or pull; spoon the wrap juices over.
st 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat in jus to ≥75 °C.

Yield | 4200
