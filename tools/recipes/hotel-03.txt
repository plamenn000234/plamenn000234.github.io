=== Celeriac Gratin · Cream, Gruyère, Thyme
at Buffet / Garnishes & Sides · N 12 · pg 150 · portions
## GRATIN
: GN 1/2, 6.5 cm
Celeriac | 1500
Potatoes | 500
Double cream | 500
Milk | 300
Garlic | 10
Thyme | 4
Nutmeg | 1
Salt | 14
White pepper | 2
Gruyère, grated | 150
Butter | 20
Yield | 2400
1. Peel and slice celeriac and potatoes 3 mm (mandoline).
2. Bring cream, milk, garlic, thyme and seasoning to the boil; add vegetables; simmer 5 min (starts the cooking, prevents splitting).
3. Layer in a buttered GN; top with cheese; bake covered 160 °C 40 min, uncovered 20 min until golden and tender. Core ≥ 75 °C.
4. Rest 15 min before cutting.
## SERVICE
1. 150 g squares with game, beef or pork.
st 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat 170 °C to ≥ 75 °C.
