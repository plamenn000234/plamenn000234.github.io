=== Beer-Braised Beef Brisket · Onions, Mustard
at Buffet / Main Courses · N 12 · pg 250 · portions
## BRAISE
Beef brisket | 3000
Salt | 30
Black pepper | 5
Sunflower oil | 50
Onion | 1000
Garlic | 30
Dark beer | 660
Beef stock | 600
Tomato paste | 40
Brown sugar | 30
Wholegrain mustard | 50
Bay leaves | 2
Thyme | 5
Yield | 3800
1. Season brisket 12 h ahead (dry brine) at 0–4 °C.
2. Sear fat-side down until deep brown; set aside. Cook sliced onions in the fat 20 min until soft and golden; add garlic and tomato paste.
3. Add beer, stock, sugar, bay and thyme; return brisket; cover tightly; braise 150 °C 3 h 30 min – 4 h until fork-tender (core ~92 °C).
4. Rest 30 min; slice across the grain 1 cm. Reduce the liquid with mustard to a sauce.
## SERVICE
1. 3 slices with onion sauce; mash or roast potatoes.
st Cook-chill whole in the liquid: 0–3 °C 4 days. Slice cold; reheat in sauce to ≥ 75 °C. Freezes −18 °C 3 months.
