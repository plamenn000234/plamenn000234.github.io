=== Midi na Tava · Pan-Roasted Black Sea Mussels · Garlic Butter, Lemon, Parsley
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 250 · plates
tag Starters & canapés / Seafood
## MUSSELS
: Live mussels, closed shells only · cooked to order
Mussels, live | 3500
Butter | 200
Garlic | 50
Black pepper | 4
Salt | 5
Parsley | 40
Lemons | 250
1. Scrub and debeard; discard broken shells and any open mussel that does not close when tapped. Keep at 2–4 °C under a damp cloth, never in water.
2. Compound butter: soften butter, mix with crushed garlic, chopped parsley and pepper; roll, chill. 25 g per portion.
## COOK TO ORDER
: Per portion · 350 g raw in a hot cast-iron pan
Yield | 2500
1. Heat a dry pan until smoking; add 350 g mussels, cover 2 min until they open (discard any that stay closed).
2. Add 25 g garlic butter, toss 30 s until the juices emulsify. Core of the meat ≥ 75 °C.
3. Serve in the pan with a lemon wedge and bread for the juices.
st Live mussels 2–4 °C, use within 48 h of delivery (keep the shellfish tag 90 days). Garlic butter 0–4 °C 7 days.

## SPRATS
: Fresh or IQF, thawed at 0–4 °C
Sprats, whole | 2000
Salt | 20
Wheat flour | 250
Cornflour | 100
Sunflower oil | 2000
1. Rinse and drain the sprats well; salt and rest 10 min at 0–4 °C; pat dry.

3. Fry at 180 °C 2–3 min until crisp and golden; drain on a rack; salt lightly. Change oil when it darkens or smokes.

Garlic | 20

Yield | 2200
1. Mix yoghurt with crushed garlic, dill and salt. Serve 50 g per portion with lemon wedges.
st Fry to order; hold max 20 min. Raw sprats 0–2 °C on ice 24 h. Yoghurt sauce 0–4 °C 48 h.
