=== Sous-Vide Egg Bites · Gruyère & Bacon · Buffet Breakfast
at Breakfast · N 24 · pg 70 · pieces
## BASE
: 24 silicone moulds × 70 g or 4 oz jars
Eggs | 1000
Cottage cheese | 300
Gruyère, grated | 200
Smoked bacon | 200
Chives | 15
Salt | 8
White pepper | 2
1. Cook diced bacon until crisp; drain.
2. Blend eggs and cottage cheese smooth 30 s (no foam: rest 10 min); add salt and pepper.
3. Divide bacon, cheese and chives into greased moulds; fill with egg mixture.
## COOK
1. Cover moulds with foil; combi oven steam 85 °C 20–25 min (or water bath 85 °C 1 h in jars). Core 75 °C.
2. Rest 3 min; unmould.
## SERVICE
1. 2 bites per guest on a hot buffet tray; refresh every 30 min.
st Cooked, chilled < 5 °C in 90 min: 0–3 °C 4 days. Reheat steam 85 °C 8 min. Hot hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h.

=== Breakfast Bread Pudding · Brioche, Berries, Vanilla Custard
at Breakfast · N 20 · pg 150 · portions

: GN 1/1, 6.5 cm
Milk | 1200
Double cream | 300
Eggs | 600
Sugar | 180
Vanilla sugar | 10

1. Whisk eggs, sugar, vanilla and cinnamon; whisk in milk and cream.
## ASSEMBLY & BAKE
Brioche, day-old | 1000
Butter | 80
Frozen mixed berries | 500
Icing sugar | 30
Yield | 3200
1. Cut brioche into 3 cm cubes; toss with melted butter; layer in a buttered GN with frozen berries.

=== Oat, Seed & Date Breakfast Bars · No Refined Sugar

Cinnamon | 4

Yield | 1500
