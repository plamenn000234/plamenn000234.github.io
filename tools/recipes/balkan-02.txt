=== Kyufteta po Chirpanski · Meatballs Braised in Tomato & Pepper Sauce · Mint, Parsley
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 350 · plates
## MEATBALLS
: 30 pieces × 45 g
Pork mince | 800
Beef mince | 500
Onion | 200
Bread, white, stale | 120
Milk 3.6% | 120
Eggs | 100
Cumin, ground | 6
Savory, dried | 4
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 20
Parsley | 30
Wheat flour | 60
Sunflower oil | 100
Yield | 1450
1. Soak bread in milk; squeeze. Grate onion and squeeze out the juice.
2. Knead mince with bread, onion, egg, spices, salt and parsley 5 min until sticky; chill 1 h at 0–4 °C.
3. Shape 30 oval balls of 45 g with wet hands; roll in flour; sear in oil on all sides (not cooked through).
## SAUCE
Onion | 250
Red peppers | 300
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 1200
Tomato paste | 40
Sunflower oil | 40
Paprika, sweet | 10
Sugar | 10
Bay leaves | 2
Water | 300
Mint, dried | 3
Salt | 10
Yield | 2000
1. Sweat onion and pepper strips in oil 10 min; add paprika off the heat, then paste, tomatoes, sugar, bay and water; simmer 15 min.
2. Lay meatballs in a GN 1/1 × 65 mm, cover with sauce; bake 170 °C 30 min. Core ≥ 75 °C.
3. Finish with dried mint and chopped parsley.
## SERVICE
1. 3 meatballs (≈ 150 g cooked) with 200 g sauce; serve with mashed potatoes or rice pilaf.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h or −18 °C 2 months; reheat covered to ≥ 75 °C. Raw mince mix 0–4 °C max 24 h.

Paprika, sweet | 6

Sunflower oil | 50
Water | 400

Mint | 15
Salt | 12

## SERVICE
Yoghurt 3.6% | 500
Yield | 4200
1. 200 g rice, 170 g lamb on top, 50 g cold yoghurt on the side.
st Lamb cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat with juices 160 °C to ≥ 75 °C. Rice: cool to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min, 0–3 °C 24 h, reheat once (Bacillus cereus).

2. Dust with flour; sear 1 min per side in hot oil (colour only).
## PLAKIA

Garlic | 25

Tomato paste | 30
White wine | 250
Sunflower oil | 100
Paprika, sweet | 10
Sugar | 10

=== Pilaf s Midi · Black Sea Mussel Pilaf · Dill, Lemon
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 350 · plates
tag Mains / Seafood
## MUSSELS
: Live mussels, closed shells only
Mussels, live | 2500
White wine | 200
Garlic | 20
Water | 300
1. Scrub and debeard; discard any open mussel that does not close when tapped.
2. Steam with wine, garlic and water covered 4–5 min until open; discard unopened. Pick the meat; strain the liquor through a cloth (≈ 600 ml).
## PILAF
Long-grain rice | 800
Onion | 300
Carrots | 150
Butter | 80
Sunflower oil | 60
Tomato paste | 30
Mussel liquor (above) | 600
Vegetable stock | 1000
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 10
Dill | 40
Lemons | 200
Yield | 3500
1. Sweat onion and grated carrot in butter and oil 6 min; add rice, toast 3 min; add paste.
2. Add hot liquor and stock; season (the liquor is salty: taste first). Cover; bake 180 °C 18 min.
3. Fold in mussel meat and dill; rest covered 8 min. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 350 g per plate with a lemon wedge.
st Serve within 60 min at ≥ 63 °C. Leftover: chill ≤ 5 °C within 90 min, 0–3 °C 24 h, reheat once to ≥ 75 °C.
