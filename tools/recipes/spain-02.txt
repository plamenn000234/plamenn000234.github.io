=== Paella Valenciana · Chicken, Rabbit, Garrofó & Green Beans · Rosemary
at Spanish & Tapas / Arroces & Paellas (Rice) · N 10 · pg 400 · plates
tag Mains / Poultry
## PAELLA
: 70–80 cm paella pan · rice is never stirred after it is spread · socarrat at the end
Chicken thighs, bone-in, jointed | 1200
Rabbit, jointed | 800
Olive oil | 200
Salt | 30
Flat green beans | 400
Garrofó (large white lima beans), cooked | 300
Tomatoes, grated | 300
Sweet paprika | 10
Saffron | 0.5
Water | 3500
Rosemary | 3
Bomba rice | 1000
Yield | 4000
1. Heat oil in the pan; salt the meat and brown it slowly on all sides 20 min, pushed to the rim.
2. Add beans in the centre 5 min; add tomato and cook until jammy; stir in paprika (do not burn).
3. Add water and saffron; boil 25 min to make the broth with the meat; correct salt (slightly over-salted). Volume ~ 3 l.
4. Scatter the rice evenly across; cook 10 min high, 8 min medium, without stirring. Lay rosemary sprigs on top 2 min before the end.
5. Raise heat 1–2 min to toast the base (socarrat); listen for crackling. Rest 5 min covered with a cloth. Chicken core ≥ 75 °C.
st Serve at once. Hot holding ≥ 63 °C max 30 min (rice softens). Leftovers: chill ≤ 5 °C within 90 min, 24 h.

=== Arroz Caldoso con Bogavante · Brothy Rice with Lobster
at Spanish & Tapas / Arroces & Paellas (Rice) · N 10 · pg 450 · bowls

## LOBSTER
: Live lobsters · chill 30 min at −18 °C to sedate before splitting
Lobster, live | 3000
Olive oil | 100
Salt | 10
1. Split lobsters lengthways; crack claws; remove head sac. Keep coral and juices.
2. Sear flesh-side down in hot oil 1 min; set aside. Keep heads and shells for the stock.
## BROTH
Lobster heads & shells (above) | 1000
Onion | 300
Green pepper | 200
Garlic | 30
Tomatoes, grated | 400
Brandy | 50
Sweet paprika | 8
Saffron | 0.5
Fish stock | 4500
1. Fry shells hard in the lobster oil; flambé with brandy; add onion, pepper and garlic 10 min, then tomato 10 min and paprika.
2. Add fish stock; simmer 30 min; press through a chinois.
## RICE
Bomba rice | 900
Lobster broth (above) | 4000
Yield | 5000
1. Bring the broth to a boil with the coral; add rice; simmer 12 min stirring occasionally.
2. Add lobster pieces; cook 4–5 min more until the rice is just tender in a loose, soupy broth. Lobster core ≥ 63 °C. Rest 2 min; serve in deep plates.
st Broth 0–3 °C 48 h or −18 °C 2 months. Cook rice to order.

=== Bacalao a la Vizcaína · Salt Cod in Choricero Pepper Sauce

## COD
Salt cod, thick loin | 1800
Olive oil | 150
Garlic | 20
1. Desalt cod in cold water at 0–4 °C 48 h, changing every 8 h. Cut into 10 portions; dry.
2. Confit skin-side up in oil with sliced garlic at 70 °C 8–10 min until flakes separate (core ≥ 63 °C).
## SAUCE
Choricero peppers, dried | 60
Onion | 1200
Garlic | 30
Olive oil | 150
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 200
Bread, day-old white | 50
Fish stock | 400
Salt | 6
Yield | 1300
1. Soak choriceros in hot water 1 h; scrape out the pulp.
2. Sweat sliced onion and garlic in oil very slowly 1 h until soft and sweet, not coloured.

## SERVICE

st Sauce 0–3 °C 5 days; freeze −18 °C 3 months. Desalted cod 0–3 °C 48 h.

=== Lechazo Asado · Castilian Roast Milk-Fed Lamb · Water, Salt, Lard
at Spanish & Tapas / Platos Principales (Mains) · N 10 · pg 350 · plates (bone-in)
tag Mains / Lamb & goat
## LAMB
: Clay dishes · wood oven if possible · only water and salt
Suckling lamb (lechazo), quarters, bone-in | 5000
Lard | 100
Salt | 50
Water | 600
Bay leaves | 2
1. Rub the lamb with lard and salt; place skin-side down in clay dishes on wooden sticks; add water and bay.
2. Roast at 160 °C 1 h 30 min, basting every 30 min; turn skin-side up.
3. Raise to 200 °C 30–40 min until the skin is golden and crisp; core ≥ 72 °C, meat falling from the bone.
4. Rest 10 min; cut into portions with a cleaver; spoon over the juices. Serve with lettuce, onion and vinegar salad.
st Serve at once. Leftovers chill ≤ 5 °C within 90 min, 0–3 °C 48 h; reheat ≥ 75 °C.
