=== Lagman · Uzbek Hand-Pulled Noodles · Lamb & Vegetable Vaju Sauce

## NOODLES
: Dough is oiled and rested twice; it pulls only when relaxed.
Strong flour | 1000
Water | 500
Salt | 12
Baking soda | 3
Sunflower oil | 60
Yield | 1500
1. Knead flour, water and salt 10 min; rest 30 min. Knead in soda dissolved in 20 g water; roll into ropes; oil; rest 1 h.
2. Pull to 3 mm; boil 3 min; rinse in warm water.
## VAJU
Lamb leg, boneless | 1200
Onion | 300
Red peppers | 300
Green peppers | 200
Carrot | 200
Daikon | 200
Tomatoes | 400
Tomato paste | 60
Garlic | 40
Cumin seeds | 6
Chilli flakes | 4
Beef stock | 1200

Sunflower oil, for sauce | 80
Yield | 3500

2. Add tomato, paste, garlic, cumin and chilli 3 min; add stock; simmer 25 min; core ≥ 75 °C; season.
3. Serve 150 g noodles with 350 g vaju.
st Vaju 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat ≥ 75 °C. Noodles to order.
