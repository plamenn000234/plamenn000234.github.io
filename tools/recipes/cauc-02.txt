=== Khashlama · Kakhetian Boiled Beef Brisket · Hot Pepper, Garlic & Fresh Herbs
at Georgian & Caucasus / Stews, Braises & Plov · N 10 · pg 300 · plates
tag Mains / Beef & veal
part Water 0.15
## BEEF
: Slow-simmered in very little seasoning; the herbs and garlic make the dish
Beef brisket | 3000
Water | 5000
Onions | 300
Bay leaves | 4
Black peppercorns | 4
Salt | 30
Yield | 2800
1. Cover the brisket with cold water; bring to the boil; skim well.
2. Add onion, bay and peppercorns; simmer at 90 °C 2 h 30 min until a skewer slides through; core ≥ 75 °C. Salt in the last 30 min.
3. Slice across the grain 1.5 cm; moisten each portion with 50 ml hot broth.
## HERBS
Garlic | 40
Green chillies | 30
Coriander, fresh | 40
Parsley, fresh | 30
Tarragon | 20
Basil | 20
Yield | 180
1. Chop herbs, garlic and chilli coarsely; scatter over the hot meat at the pass. Serve with shoti bread and tkemali.
st Meat in broth cooled within 90 min, 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat in broth to ≥ 75 °C. Herbs cut to order.

=== Chanakhi · Georgian Lamb & Aubergine Clay-Pot Stew · Tomato, Basil & Coriander

tag Mains / Lamb & goat

: Layered raw and baked slowly; no stirring
Lamb shoulder | 1800
Sunflower oil | 40
Onions | 400
Aubergines | 1200
Potatoes, waxy | 1000
Green peppers | 300
Tomatoes | 1200
Garlic | 40
Coriander, fresh | 40
Basil | 30
Salt | 30
Black pepper | 4
Yield | 4500
1. Cut lamb in 3 cm cubes; season; brown lightly in oil. Salt sliced aubergine 20 min; squeeze.
2. In each pot layer: lamb, onion, potato, aubergine, pepper, chopped herbs and garlic, then grated tomato on top. Season every layer.

=== Khash · Armenian Beef Foot Broth · Garlic, Dried Lavash & Radish
at Georgian & Caucasus / Khinkali, Dumplings & Soups · N 12 · pg 400 · bowls
tag Soups / Beef & veal
part Beef feet 0.3
## BROTH
: Winter morning dish; salt and garlic are added at the table
Beef feet, split | 4000
Beef tripe | 1000
Water | 8000
Yield | 3700
1. Singe and scrape the feet; soak with the tripe 24 h in cold water at 0–3 °C, changing it 3 times.
2. Cover with fresh water; bring to the boil; skim; simmer at 90 °C 8 h, topping up with boiling water, until the meat leaves the bones and the broth is milky.
3. Lift out; take off the meat; cut tripe in strips; return to the broth. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## TABLE
Garlic | 150
Salt | 30
Lavash, dried | 600
Radishes | 300
Coriander, fresh | 30
Yield | 1110
1. Crush garlic with a little salt and broth. Crumble dried lavash into the bowls; serve with garlic, salt, radishes and herbs.
st Broth 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Freeze −18 °C 3 months.

=== Shotis Puri · Georgian Canoe Bread · Long-Ferment White Dough
at Georgian & Caucasus / Khachapuri, Breads & Pies · N 10 · pg 225 · breads
tag Bread & bakery / -
## DOUGH
: Traditionally slapped onto the wall of a tone; a deck oven with a stone works
Strong flour | 1500
Water | 1000
Fresh yeast | 15
Salt | 30
Yield | 2250
1. Mix flour, water and yeast; rest 30 min; add salt; knead 8 min (dough 24 °C).
2. Bulk ferment 2 h with 2 folds, then 0–3 °C overnight.
3. Divide 10 × 250 g; pre-shape; rest 30 min. Stretch into 40 cm canoes with pointed ends and a thick central rim.
4. Bake on the stone at 250 °C with steam 12–14 min until blistered and golden; core 96 °C.
st Room temperature 24 h; freeze −18 °C 1 month.
