=== Pinnekjøtt · Norwegian Salted & Dried Lamb Ribs · Steamed over Birch

Water | 6000
## MASH
Swede | 2000
Carrots | 500
Butter | 150
Cream 33% | 150
Salt | 6
Nutmeg | 0.5
Yield | 5000
1. Cut ribs between the bones; soak in cold water 30 h at 0–3 °C, changing the water twice.
2. Steam on a rack (birch sticks or a grid) over water, covered, 2 h 30 min until the meat leaves the bone (core ≥ 75 °C); top up water.
