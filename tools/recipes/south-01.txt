## KARAHI
Chicken thighs, bone-in | 2500
Ghee | 150
Tomatoes, ripe | 1200
Garlic | 50
Ginger, fresh | 60
Green chillies | 60
Chilli flakes | 6
Cumin seeds | 5
Coriander seed | 6
Black pepper | 4
Salt | 25
Greek yogurt | 150
Coriander, fresh | 40
Yield | 3600
1. Sear chicken pieces in ghee in a karahi over high heat 8 min. Add garlic and half the ginger paste 2 min.
2. Add halved tomatoes; cover 5 min; pull off the skins; mash into the pan. Cook uncovered 15 min until the oil separates.
