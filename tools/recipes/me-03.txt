=== Madfoon · Yemeni Pit-Style Slow-Roast Lamb · Hawaij Rice & Tomato Salsa
at Middle Eastern / Ṭabīkh & Ṭawājin (Stews, Tagines & Mains) · N 10 · pg 500 · plates
tag Mains / Lamb & goat
## LAMB
Lamb shoulder, bone-in | 3500
Hawaij spice blend | 30
Garlic | 40
Salt | 35
Black pepper | 5
Ghee | 60
Yield | 2200
1. Rub lamb with hawaij, crushed garlic, salt, pepper and ghee; marinate 12 h at 0–3 °C.
2. Roast sealed in foil over a rack of water at 150 °C 4 h 30 min until falling apart; core ≥ 75 °C. Uncover at 220 °C 15 min.
## RICE
Basmati rice | 900
Onion | 300
Hawaij spice blend, for rice | 15
Cardamom, green | 4
Ghee, for rice | 60
Lamb stock | 1300
Salt, for rice | 10
Yield | 2400
1. Fry onion in ghee until golden; add spices and rice; add stock and salt; cook covered 18 min.
2. Set the rack of lamb over the rice for the last 30 min so juices drip in.
## SAHAWIQ SALSA
Tomatoes | 400
Green chillies | 30
Coriander, fresh | 30
Garlic, sliced | 10
Salt, for salsa | 4
Yield | 450
1. Pound to a coarse salsa.
st Lamb 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat ≥ 75 °C. Rice cool < 2 h; 0–3 °C 24 h.

=== Bint al Sahn · Yemeni Honey Layer Bread · Black Seed & Samn
at Middle Eastern / Ḥalawiyyāt & Mashrūbāt (Desserts & Drinks) · N 12 · pg 100 · wedges
tag Desserts / -
## DOUGH
Plain flour | 600
Eggs | 150
Whole milk | 180
Yeast, dried | 6
Sugar | 20
Salt | 6
Ghee | 250
Nigella seeds | 4
Honey | 200
Yield | 1300
1. Knead flour, eggs, milk, yeast, sugar and salt 10 min; rest 1 h.
2. Divide into 12; roll paper-thin; brush each with ghee and stack in a 28 cm tin; brush top with egg and sprinkle nigella.

tag Bread & bakery / -
## DOUGH
: 10 rolls × 130 g. Baked overnight in a sealed pot.
Plain flour | 900
Water | 500
Sugar | 60
Honey | 30
Salt | 15
Butter, soft | 250
Yield | 1500
1. Knead flour, water, sugar, honey and salt 10 min; divide into 10; rest oiled 1 h.
2. Stretch each paper-thin on a buttered bench; brush butter; fold in thirds; roll up tightly into a log.
3. Pack in a lined pot; seal; bake 105 °C 10 h.

Eggs | 600
Tomatoes | 500
Zhoug | 150
Salt, for tomato | 4
Yield | 1100
1. Bake whole eggs alongside the rolls (huevos haminados style); grate tomato with salt. Serve with zhoug.
st Baked rolls 0–3 °C 48 h; rewarm 160 °C 10 min.
