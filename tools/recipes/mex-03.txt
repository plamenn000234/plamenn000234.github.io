=== Chileatole · Puebla Green Corn & Chilli Masa Soup · Epazote & Fresh Corn

Masa harina | 120
Water | 2200
Vegetable stock | 600
Poblano peppers | 300
Green chillies | 30
Onion | 150
Garlic | 15
Epazote (or coriander) | 20
Coriander, fresh | 30
Sunflower oil | 30
Salt | 16
Yield | 3800
1. Char poblanos; peel and seed. Blend with green chilli, coriander, epazote and 500 g water.
