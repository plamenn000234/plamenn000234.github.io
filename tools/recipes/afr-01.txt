=== Efo Riro · Nigerian Spinach Stew · Pepper Base, Smoked Fish & Beef

tag Mains / Beef & veal

Onion | 300
Salt | 16
Water | 800
Red peppers | 600
Scotch bonnet chillies | 30
Tomatoes | 400
Palm oil | 150
Smoked mackerel, flaked | 300
Locust bean (iru), fermented | 30
Spinach | 2000
Crayfish, ground dried | 40
Yield | 3700
1. Simmer beef in 3 cm cubes with half the onion, salt and water 1 h; keep the stock.
2. Blend peppers, chilli, tomatoes and remaining onion; boil down 20 min until thick.
3. Heat palm oil gently (do not smoke); fry iru 1 min; add pepper base and fry 15 min until the oil separates.

2. Coarsely blend peppers, chillies and half the onion; boil until the water is gone.
