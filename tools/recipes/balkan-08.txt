=== Ciorbă de Perișoare · Romanian Meatball Sour Soup · Borș, Lovage
at Adriatic & Balkan / Romania · N 12 · pg 380 · bowls
## MEATBALLS
: 60 balls × 20 g
Pork & beef mince | 900
Long-grain rice | 100
Onion | 120
Eggs | 100
Dill | 15
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 10
1. Grate onion and squeeze; mix with mince, washed raw rice, eggs, chopped dill, pepper and salt; knead 3 min until tacky.
2. Roll 20 g balls with wet hands; keep at 0–4 °C until the soup boils.
## SOUP
: 10 L pot · ≈ 4.6 kg finished
Water | 3500
Carrots | 250
Parsnip | 150
Celeriac | 150
Onion | 200
Red peppers | 150
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 300
Borș (fermented bran sour) | 600
Sunflower oil | 40
Salt | 15
Lovage | 15
Yield | 4600
1. Sweat onion, carrot, parsnip and celeriac (1 cm dice) in oil 8 min; add water, bring to the boil.
2. Drop the meatballs into the simmering stock one by one; simmer 25 min without stirring hard, skimming.
3. Add peppers and tomatoes 10 min. Boil the borș separately (removes raw taste), add to the soup, simmer 3 min.
4. Season, finish with chopped lovage. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 380 g with 5 meatballs; sour cream and hot pepper on the side.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Add fresh lovage after reheating.

=== Ciorbă de Burtă · Romanian Tripe Soup · Garlic, Sour Cream & Yolk Liaison
at Adriatic & Balkan / Romania · N 12 · pg 380 · bowls
## TRIPE & STOCK
: Day 1 · tripe cooked ≈ 4 h
Beef tripe, cleaned | 1500
Beef bones | 1500
Water | 5000
Carrots | 200
Parsnip | 150
Onion | 150
Salt | 15
1. Wash the tripe in several waters; blanch from cold 10 min, rinse.
2. Simmer tripe and blanched bones with vegetables at 90 °C 3 h 30–4 h until the tripe is tender; skim regularly.
3. Strain (≈ 3.2 L stock); cut tripe into 5 × 0.5 cm strips. Cool stock and tripe separately to < 5 °C within 2 h if held overnight.
## LIAISON & FINISH
Beef stock (above) | 3200
Tripe, cooked (above) | 1200
Sour cream 20% | 500
Egg yolks | 120
Garlic | 60
White wine vinegar | 60
Salt | 8
Yield | 4800
1. Bring the stock and tripe back to the boil.
2. Whisk sour cream and yolks; temper with 600 ml hot stock.
3. Off the heat, stir in the liaison; return to 80–85 °C, stirring, 3 min. Do not boil (curdles).
4. Season with vinegar, salt and the crushed garlic paste (mujdei) to a clear sour-garlic taste.
## SERVICE
1. 380 g in a hot bowl; extra vinegar, mujdei and hot green pepper on the side.
st Tripe and stock: 0–3 °C 72 h. Finished soup with liaison: hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h; reheat once only, gently, to ≥ 75 °C.

=== Salată de Vinete · Romanian Smoked Aubergine Salad · Onion, Sunflower Oil
at Adriatic & Balkan / Romania · N 12 · pg 100 · portions
## AUBERGINE
: Charred whole on flame · cooked flesh ≈ 55 %
Aubergines | 2200
1. Char aubergines whole on a gas flame or grill until black and collapsed, 15–20 min, turning.
2. Peel while hot on a wooden board, drain vertically in a colander 30 min (bitter juice).
3. Chop with a wooden or blunt knife into a coarse paste (metal darkens the flesh, traditional practice).
## DRESSING
Sunflower oil | 150
Onion | 120
Lemon juice | 30
Salt | 10
Yield | 1200
1. Beat the oil into the aubergine little by little with a wooden spoon, like a mayonnaise, until pale and creamy.
2. Fold in finely chopped onion, lemon and salt.
## SERVICE
1. 100 g with tomato slices and bread. A mayonnaise variant (add 100 g) is common; adjust allergens if used.
st Chilled 0–4 °C 48 h, surface covered with cling film (oxidises).

=== Saramură de Pește · Danube-Style Fish in Brine · Garlic, Chilli, Tomato
at Adriatic & Balkan / Romania · N 10 · pg 350 · plates
## FISH
: Whole carp or pike-perch, scored · grill 200 °C
Carp fillets, skin-on | 2000
Salt | 25
Sunflower oil | 40
1. Pin-bone fillets; score the skin every 1 cm (cuts fine bones). Salt 20 min before cooking.
2. Grill skin-side down on a very hot grill 6–8 min, turn, 3 min. Core 60 °C.
## SARAMURĂ
Water | 1200
Salt | 25
Tomatoes | 400
Red peppers | 200
Garlic | 50
Hot chilli peppers | 20
Bay leaves | 2
Black peppercorns | 3
Thyme | 5
Parsley | 20
1. Simmer water with salt, bay and peppercorns 5 min.
2. Add diced tomatoes, peppers, crushed garlic and chilli; simmer 8 min. Finish with thyme and parsley.
## SERVICE
1. Place hot fish in a deep plate, ladle 150 ml boiling saramură over; serve at once with mămăligă.
st Brine 0–4 °C 48 h. Fish is grilled to order; raw fillets 0–2 °C 24 h.

=== Bulz Ciobănesc · Shepherd's Mămăligă Balls · Telemea, Sour Cream, Fried Egg

## MĂMĂLIGĂ
: Firm polenta · 1 : 3.5
Water | 1750
Cornmeal | 500
Salt | 12
Butter | 50
1. Boil salted water; rain in cornmeal whisking; cook on low heat 30 min, stirring with a wooden spoon, until it pulls from the pot.

## FILLING
Telemea (sheep cheese) | 400
Smoked bacon | 300
1. Crumble cheese; dice bacon and render until crisp.

## BAKE & FINISH
Butter | 60
Eggs | 600
Sour cream 20% | 500
1. Brush balls with melted butter; bake 220 °C 12–15 min until crusted, core ≥ 75 °C.
2. Fry eggs sunny-side to order.
## SERVICE
1. 2 bulz per plate, one fried egg on top, 50 g sour cream on the side.
st Shaped raw balls 0–4 °C 48 h. Bake to order.

=== Plăcintă cu Brânză Dulce · Moldavian Sweet Cheese Pie · Raisins, Lemon Zest
at Adriatic & Balkan / Romania · N 20 · pg 120 · portions
## DOUGH
: Short yeast dough · GN 1/1
Wheat flour | 700
Milk | 250
Butter | 150
Eggs | 100
Sugar | 60
Fresh yeast | 25
Salt | 5
1. Warm milk to 30 °C, dissolve yeast and sugar; mix in flour, eggs, salt and soft butter; knead 8 min. Prove 45 min at 26 °C.
## FILLING
Cottage cheese | 1500
Sugar | 250
Eggs | 300
Sour cream 20% | 200
Semolina | 60
Raisins | 150
Lemon zest | 10
Vanilla sugar | 10
Yield | 3200
1. Press cottage cheese through a sieve; mix with sugar, eggs, sour cream, semolina, zest and soaked raisins.
## ASSEMBLY & BAKE
Egg yolks | 40
Icing sugar | 30
1. Roll 2/3 of the dough to line a greased GN 1/1; spread filling; lattice the rest of the dough on top; brush with yolk.
2. Prove 15 min; bake 170 °C 45–50 min until set (core ≥ 75 °C). Cool in the tray; dust with icing sugar.
## SERVICE
1. 20 portions, room temperature or slightly warm.
st 0–4 °C 72 h, covered. Freezes −18 °C 1 month.
