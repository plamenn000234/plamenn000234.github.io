=== Aligot · Aubrac Cheese & Garlic Potato Purée · Tomme Fraîche
at French / Légumes & Garnitures (Vegetables & Sides) · N 12 · pg 200 · portions

## ALIGOT
: Beat hard and long so the cheese stretches in ribbons
Potatoes, floury | 1500
Butter | 120
Cream 35% | 250
Garlic | 15
Tomme fraîche (or young cantal / mozzarella) | 800
Salt | 12
White pepper | 1
Yield | 2500
1. Steam or boil potatoes; dry; pass through a ricer.
2. Return to a heavy pan over low heat with butter, garlic crushed into the cream, and salt.
