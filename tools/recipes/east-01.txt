=== Żurek · Polish Sour Rye Soup · White Sausage, Egg, Marjoram
at Eastern European / Soups · N 12 · pg 380 · bowls
## ZAKWAS (RYE SOUR)
: Start 4–5 days ahead · 20–24 °C
Wholemeal rye flour | 150
Water | 750
Garlic | 15
Bay leaves | 1
1. Mix rye flour with lukewarm boiled water, garlic and bay in a glass jar; cover with cloth.
2. Ferment 4–5 days at 20–24 °C, stirring daily, until pleasantly sour. Strain; keep 0–4 °C up to 7 days.
## SOUP
Water | 3000
Biała kiełbasa (white sausage) | 800
Smoked bacon | 200
Onion | 200
Carrots | 150
Parsnip | 100
Celeriac | 100
Potatoes | 600
Rye sour (above) | 700
Sour cream 20% | 200
Garlic | 20
Marjoram, dried | 4
Horseradish, grated | 30
Salt | 12
Black pepper | 3
Yield | 4600
1. Simmer whole sausages 20 min in the water at 85 °C (do not boil: skins split); lift out and slice.
2. Add bacon and vegetables, simmer 20 min; add diced potatoes 15 min.
3. Shake the rye sour, pour in while stirring; simmer 5 min until it thickens.
4. Temper sour cream; stir in off the boil with crushed garlic, marjoram, horseradish, salt and pepper. Return sausage. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
Eggs | 360
1. Hard-boil eggs 10 min, halve. Serve 380 g with sausage slices and half an egg per bowl (or in a bread bowl).
st Rye sour 0–4 °C 7 days. Soup 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C without boiling hard.

=== Ukha · Russian Clear Fish Soup · Pike-Perch, Vodka, Dill
at Eastern European / Soups · N 10 · pg 380 · bowls
## FISH STOCK
: Heads, bones and small river fish · 30 min only
Fish heads and bones, white fish | 1500
Water | 3500
Onion | 150
Parsley root | 80
Bay leaves | 2
Black peppercorns | 3
1. Remove gills; soak heads 20 min in cold water. Cover with water, bring slowly to 85 °C, skim.
2. Add onion, parsley root, bay and peppercorns; simmer 30 min (longer clouds and bitters). Strain through muslin.
## SOUP
Fish stock (above) | 3000
Pike-perch fillets | 1200
Potatoes | 600
Carrots | 150
Onion | 100
Vodka | 40
Dill | 30
Chives | 15
Salt | 18
Yield | 4600
1. Simmer potatoes (2 cm dice), carrot and onion in the stock 12 min.
2. Add fillets in 50 g pieces; poach at 80 °C 6–7 min to core 60 °C.
3. Stir in vodka, season; add herbs off the heat.
## SERVICE
1. 380 g with 2–3 pieces of fish; rye bread.
st Stock 0–3 °C 48 h. Finish fish to order or within 1 h; hold ≥ 63 °C max 1 h.

Potatoes | 500
Tomato paste | 60
Sunflower oil | 50
Bay leaves | 2
Garlic | 20

Black pepper | 3
Yield | 4600
