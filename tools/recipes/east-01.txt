=== Rassolnik · Russian Pickle & Barley Soup · Beef, Smetana
at Eastern European / Soups · N 12 · pg 350 · bowls
tag Soups / Beef & veal
## SOUP
Beef shin, boneless | 800
Pearl barley | 150
Pickled cucumbers | 400
Pickle brine | 300
Potatoes | 600
Onion | 250
Carrots | 200
Parsley root | 100
Sunflower oil | 60
Tomato paste | 30
Water | 2800
Bay leaves | 2
Black peppercorns | 4
Salt | 10
## FINISH
Sour cream 20% | 240
Dill, fresh | 25
Yield | 4200
1. Simmer the beef in the water with bay and pepper 1 h 30 min; skim. Lift out, dice 1.5 cm; strain the broth.
2. Soak barley 1 h; cook separately 40 min; drain (keeps the soup clear).
3. Sweat onion, carrot and parsley root in oil 8 min; add tomato paste and the cucumbers in strips; cook 5 min.
4. Bring the broth back to the boil; add potatoes in 1.5 cm dice; cook 10 min; add barley, vegetables, beef and brine to taste; simmer 10 min. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 330 g per bowl; 20 g smetana; dill.
st Hot hold ≥ 63 °C max 3 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C.
