Salt | 10

## KACHAMAK
: Heavy pot · 30 min stirring
Cornmeal, medium | 700
Water | 2800
Salt | 20
Butter | 80
1. Bring salted water to a boil; rain in cornmeal while whisking; cook on low heat 30 min, stirring with a wooden paddle, until it pulls away from the sides.

## TOPPING
Sirene (white cheese) | 500
Pork fatback | 400
Paprika, sweet | 6
Yield | 3000

=== Tutmanik · Sirene & Yoghurt Pull-Apart Bread · Butter Crust

Yoghurt 3.6% | 250
Milk 3.6% | 200
Eggs | 100
Fresh yeast | 25
Sugar | 15
Salt | 10
Sunflower oil | 60
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm milk (35 °C); mix with yoghurt, egg and oil; knead with flour and salt 8 min to a soft dough. Prove 1 h at 26 °C.
## FILLING
Sirene (white cheese) | 400
Eggs | 100
Butter | 80
Yield | 1500

Vegetable stock | 1000
Paprika, sweet | 4

Parsley, fresh | 25
Yield | 1800
