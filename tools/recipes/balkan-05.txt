=== Garash Torta · Walnut Meringue & Chocolate Ganache Cake · Ruse Classic
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 16 · pg 110 · portions
tag Desserts
## WALNUT MERINGUE LAYERS
: 5 discs Ø 24 cm on baking paper
Egg whites | 400
Sugar | 400
Walnuts, ground | 400
Wheat flour | 40
1. Whip whites to soft peaks; add sugar in 3 additions to a stiff glossy meringue.
2. Fold in finely ground walnuts mixed with flour by hand.
3. Spread 5 thin discs; bake 160 °C 15–18 min until light golden; cool on the paper.
## GANACHE
Double cream | 600
Dark chocolate 55% | 500
Butter | 60
1. Bring cream to 85 °C; pour over chopped chocolate; rest 1 min; emulsify from the centre; add soft butter at 40 °C.
2. Cool to 26–28 °C (spreading consistency).
## ASSEMBLY
Walnuts, halves | 60
Yield | 1800
1. Layer discs and ganache (≈ 120 g between layers); coat top and sides; decorate with walnut halves.
2. Set at 0–4 °C 6 h before cutting with a hot knife into 16 portions.
st 0–4 °C 4 days covered. Freezes −18 °C 1 month (thaw 12 h at 0–4 °C).

## CHOUX
Water | 500
Butter | 80
Salt | 3
Wheat flour | 350
Eggs | 300
Sunflower oil | 2000
Yield | 1500
1. Boil water, butter and salt; add flour at once and cook 2 min stirring until a film forms on the pan.
2. Cool 5 min; beat in eggs one by one to a smooth piping consistency.
3. Pipe 6 cm fingers through a 12 mm star tip into oil at 160 °C (start cooler for a hollow centre); fry 6–8 min turning, until deep golden and dry.
4. Drop hot tolumbichki into the cold syrup 3–4 min; lift and drain.

## CARAMEL
Sugar | 250
Water | 60
1. Cook sugar and water to a deep amber (180 °C); pour 25 g into each 150 ml ramekin; let set.
## CUSTARD
Milk 3.6% | 1000
Eggs | 400
Egg yolks | 80
Sugar | 180
Vanilla pod | 2
Yield | 1600
1. Scald milk with the scraped vanilla to 85 °C; pour onto eggs, yolks and sugar whisked together (no foam). Strain; rest 10 min; skim.
2. Fill ramekins; bake in a 50 °C water bath at 150 °C 40–45 min until set with a slight wobble (core 80 °C).
3. Cool; chill at least 6 h at 0–4 °C. Unmould with a knife around the edge onto a plate with a rim.
st 0–4 °C 72 h in the ramekins, covered.

=== Boza · Fermented Millet Drink · Lightly Sparkling
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 20 · pg 250 · glasses
tag Drinks
## BOZA
: Start 3 days ahead · keep the starter from the last batch
Millet flour | 300
Wheat flour | 100
Water | 5000
Sugar | 600
Boza starter | 300
Yield | 5000
1. Toast flours in a dry pan to light beige; whisk into 4 L cold water; cook 30 min, stirring, to a thin porridge.
2. Add 1 L cold water; cool to 30 °C. Strain through a fine sieve.
3. Stir in the sugar and the starter; ferment covered at 24–26 °C for 24–36 h until slightly sour and fizzy.
4. Chill at once to 0–4 °C to slow fermentation; keep 200 ml as the next starter.
st 0–4 °C 5 days in closed containers (pressure builds; vent daily). Serve at 4–6 °C, stirred.
