1. Cream butter and half the sugar; beat in yolks. Whip whites with the rest of the sugar to soft peaks.
2. Fold buckwheat, almonds and baking powder into the butter mix, then the whites.
3. Bake in a 26 cm tin at 170 °C 50 min (core ≥ 92 °C). Cool.
## FILLING
