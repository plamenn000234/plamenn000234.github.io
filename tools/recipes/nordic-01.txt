=== Rullepølse · Danish Rolled Spiced Pork Belly · Sliced for Smørrebrød

tag Starters & canapés / Pork
part Pork belly 0.8
## ROLL
Pork belly, boneless, skin off | 2500
Salt | 50
Curing salt (nitrite) | 6
Sugar | 15
Onion | 200
Allspice, ground | 3
Black pepper | 4
Parsley | 40
Gelatine leaves | 10
Water | 4000
Bay leaves | 3
Yield | 1800
1. Butterfly the belly to an even 2.5 cm sheet; rub with salt, curing salt and sugar; cure 3 days at 0–3 °C, vacuum-packed.
2. Rinse; scatter grated onion, allspice, pepper, chopped parsley and soaked gelatine; roll tightly and tie or net.
3. Simmer in water with bay at 85 °C for 2 h 30 min (core ≥ 72 °C).
4. Press overnight under weight at 0–3 °C; slice 2 mm thin.
st 0–3 °C 7 days vacuum-packed; sliced 48 h.
