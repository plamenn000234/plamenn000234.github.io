=== Bougatsa me Krema · Thessaloniki Semolina Custard Filo Pie · Icing Sugar, Cinnamon

tag Breakfast
## CUSTARD
Milk | 1500
Sugar | 250
Fine semolina | 180
Eggs | 300
Butter | 80
Vanilla | 3
1. Bring milk and half the sugar to the boil; rain in semolina while whisking; cook 5 min to a thick cream.
2. Off the heat beat in butter; temper eggs whisked with the rest of the sugar and vanilla; return to the heat 1 min without boiling (≥ 75 °C). Cover the surface; cool to 40 °C.
## PASTRY
Filo pastry | 500
Butter, clarified | 200
Icing sugar | 40
Cinnamon | 4
Yield | 2400
1. Brush a 40 × 30 cm tray; lay 6 sheets of filo, buttering each and letting them ruffle slightly.
2. Spread the custard; cover with 6 more buttered sheets; score into 16 without cutting through.
3. Bake 180 °C 40–45 min until deep golden and crisp. Rest 15 min.
4. Cut; dust heavily with icing sugar and cinnamon. Serve warm.
st Same day for crisp pastry; custard 0–4 °C 24 h.
