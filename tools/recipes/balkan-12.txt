2. Mix cornmeal, flour, soda and salt; add yoghurt, water, eggs and remaining oil to a thick batter. Fold in the greens.
3. Spread 3 cm thick in an oiled 30 × 40 cm tray; bake 200 °C 35–40 min until golden and set. Core ≥ 75 °C. Cut into 16.
## SERVICE
1. 120 g warm, with yoghurt.
st Room temperature 12 h. 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat at 180 °C.
