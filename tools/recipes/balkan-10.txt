=== Bograč · Prekmurje Three-Meat Goulash · Paprika, Potato, Red Wine
at Adriatic & Balkan / Slovenia · N 12 · pg 400 · bowls
## MEAT
: Equal parts beef, pork, venison
Beef shin, boneless | 800
Pork shoulder, boneless | 800
Venison shoulder, boneless | 800
Lard | 80
Salt | 20
1. Cut all meat into 2.5 cm cubes; brown in lard in batches; season.
## GOULASH
Onion | 1000
Garlic | 40
Paprika, sweet | 40
Paprika, hot | 6
Caraway seeds | 4
Tomato paste | 80
Dry red wine | 400
Water | 2000
Bay leaves | 3
Potatoes | 1000
Red peppers | 300
Marjoram, dried | 4
Yield | 5000
1. Cook finely chopped onion in the fat 20 min until soft and golden (body of the sauce).
2. Off the heat add paprikas, caraway and garlic; add tomato paste, deglaze with wine.
3. Return meat, water and bay; simmer covered 1 h 30 min (venison and beef first, pork after 30 min if cut large).
4. Add potatoes (3 cm) and peppers; simmer 25 min; finish with marjoram. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 400 g in a hot bowl with bread.
st Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; better the next day. Reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Freezes −18 °C 3 months (without potato best).

=== Ričet · Slovenian Barley & Bean Stew · Smoked Pork
at Adriatic & Balkan / Slovenia · N 12 · pg 380 · bowls
## STEW
: Beans soaked 12 h
Pearl barley | 400
Dried borlotti beans | 400
Smoked pork hock | 1200
Onion | 300
Carrots | 300
Celeriac | 200
Potatoes | 600
Garlic | 30
Tomato paste | 40
Lard | 50
Water | 4500
Bay leaves | 2
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 10
Parsley | 20
Yield | 4600
1. Soak beans 12 h. Simmer hock and beans in water 1 h.
2. Add washed barley; simmer 30 min.
3. Sweat onion, carrot and celeriac in lard 8 min; add garlic and tomato paste; add to the pot with diced potatoes; simmer 25 min until thick.
4. Lift out hock, take meat off the bone, dice, return. Season; parsley. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 380 g with rye bread; a dash of cider vinegar optional.
st Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h (thickens; add water on reheating to ≥ 75 °C).

=== Orehova Potica · Slovenian Walnut Roll · Honey, Yeast Dough
at Adriatic & Balkan / Slovenia · N 24 · pg 80 · slices
## DOUGH
: 2 ring moulds Ø 24 cm or 2 loaf tins
Wheat flour | 700
Milk | 300
Butter | 120
Egg yolks | 80
Sugar | 80
Fresh yeast | 30
Salt | 6
Lemon zest | 5
1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk (30 °C) with 1 tsp sugar; mix with flour, yolks, sugar, zest, salt; add soft butter; knead 10 min to a silky dough.
2. Prove 1 h at 26 °C until doubled.
## FILLING
Walnuts, ground | 600
Milk | 250
Honey | 150
Sugar | 150
Butter | 80
Eggs | 100
Cinnamon | 4
Rum | 30
1. Bring milk, honey, sugar and butter to the boil; pour over the walnuts; cool to 30 °C; mix in eggs, cinnamon and rum.
## ASSEMBLY & BAKE
Egg yolks | 30
Yield | 2200
1. Roll dough on a floured cloth to 3 mm; spread filling evenly, leaving 2 cm edges; roll up tightly with the cloth.
2. Place in greased moulds; prove 40 min; brush with yolk; prick with a skewer.
3. Bake 170 °C 50–60 min (core 95 °C). Cool 10 min in the mould, then on a rack.
## SERVICE
1. 2 cm slices; dust with icing sugar if wanted.
st Room temperature, wrapped, 5 days. Freezes −18 °C 2 months.

=== Ajdova Kaša z Jurčki · Buckwheat with Porcini · Sour Cream
at Adriatic & Balkan / Slovenia · N 10 · pg 200 · portions
## KAŠA
: Toasted buckwheat · 1 : 2
Buckwheat groats | 500
Water | 1000
Salt | 10
Butter | 40
1. Toast buckwheat dry in a pan 4 min until nutty; add boiling salted water and butter; cover, simmer on low 15 min; rest 10 min off the heat.
## MUSHROOMS
Porcini, fresh | 600
Onion | 200
Butter | 80
Garlic | 15
Parsley | 20
Black pepper | 2
Salt | 6
Sour cream 20% | 200
Yield | 2200
1. Slice porcini; sauté in hot butter in batches until golden; add onion and garlic 3 min; season.
2. Fold into the buckwheat with parsley.
## SERVICE
1. 200 g as a side for game or pork, or as a vegetarian main with 20 g sour cream on top.
st Cooked kaša 0–4 °C 72 h; reheat with butter to ≥ 75 °C. Fresh porcini 0–4 °C 3 days, dry.

=== Tavë Kosi · Albanian Baked Lamb in Yoghurt · Rice, Oregano
at Adriatic & Balkan / Albania · N 10 · pg 400 · plates
## LAMB
: Bone-in shoulder pieces
Lamb shoulder, bone-in | 2500
Butter | 80
Long-grain rice | 150
Garlic | 30
Oregano, dried | 4
Salt | 20
Black pepper | 4
Water | 600
1. Cut lamb into 10 portions; season; brown in butter.
2. Place in a GN or clay dish with garlic, oregano and water; cover; bake 180 °C 1 h 15 min until tender.
3. Scatter washed rice around the meat; bake covered 20 min more.
## YOGHURT CUSTARD
Yoghurt 3.6% | 1500
Eggs | 400
Wheat flour | 60
Butter | 60
Nutmeg | 1
Salt | 6
Yield | 4400
1. Cook flour in butter 1 min, cool slightly; whisk into the yoghurt with eggs, salt and nutmeg.
2. Pour over the lamb; bake uncovered 180 °C 35–40 min until set and golden; core ≥ 75 °C. Rest 10 min.
## SERVICE
1. One portion of lamb with custard and rice.
st Cook-chill 0–3 °C 48 h; reheat covered 170 °C to ≥ 75 °C (custard is best fresh).

=== Fërgesë · Albanian Peppers, Tomato & Curd Cheese · Baked in Clay
at Adriatic & Balkan / Albania · N 12 · pg 180 · portions
## FËRGESË
Red peppers | 1500
Tomatoes | 1000
Onion | 300
Garlic | 30
Olive oil | 150
Cottage cheese | 800
Feta | 300
Black pepper | 3
Chilli flakes | 3
Salt | 6
Yield | 2200
1. Roast peppers 230 °C 25 min; peel and seed; cut into strips.
2. Sweat onion and garlic in oil 8 min; add peeled diced tomatoes; cook down 15 min.
3. Add peppers, then both cheeses; stir over low heat until creamy (cheese should not split).
4. Transfer to clay dishes; bake 200 °C 12–15 min until bubbling. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 180 g warm in the dish with bread, as a starter or with grilled meat.
st 0–4 °C 72 h; reheat in the dish 200 °C to ≥ 75 °C.
