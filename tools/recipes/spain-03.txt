=== Potaje de Vigilia · Lenten Chickpea, Spinach & Salt Cod Stew · Egg
at Spanish & Tapas / Sopas & Guisos (Soups & Stews) · N 12 · pg 350 · bowls
tag Soups / Legumes
## CHICKPEAS
: Soak chickpeas 12 h · add to boiling water so they stay whole
Chickpeas, dried | 800
Water | 4000
Bay leaves | 2
Onion | 150
1. Soak chickpeas 12 h in cold water at 0–4 °C; drain.
2. Add to boiling water with bay and half onion; simmer 1 h 30 min until creamy; do not salt yet.
## STEW
Salt cod, desalted | 600
Spinach | 800
Olive oil | 120
Onion | 200
Garlic | 30
Bread, fried | 40
Sweet paprika | 10
Eggs | 360
Salt | 10
Yield | 4200
1. Desalt cod 36 h at 0–4 °C, changing water; flake.
2. Fry onion and garlic in oil; pound with fried bread and paprika to a paste (majado).
3. Add the majado, spinach and cod to the chickpeas; simmer 15 min. Correct salt (the cod adds some).
4. Boil eggs 10 min, quarter; garnish each bowl.
st 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat once to ≥ 75 °C.

=== Lentejas Estofadas · Spanish Lentil Stew · Chorizo, Pepper, Paprika
at Spanish & Tapas / Sopas & Guisos (Soups & Stews) · N 12 · pg 350 · bowls
tag Soups / Legumes
## LENTILS
Lentils, brown (pardina) | 800
Water | 3500
Chorizo, cooking | 400
Onion | 300
Green pepper | 150
Carrot | 200
Potatoes | 500
Garlic | 30
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 200
Bay leaves | 2
Olive oil | 100
Sweet paprika | 10
Salt | 22
Yield | 4400
1. Rinse lentils; start in cold water with whole onion half, bay, carrot and chorizo; bring to a simmer.
2. Fry chopped onion, pepper and garlic in oil 15 min; add tomato 10 min, then paprika off the heat; add to the pot.
3. Simmer 30 min; add potatoes cut 'chascadas' (broken, not sliced) 20 min more until creamy. Salt at the end.
4. Slice chorizo back into the pot.
st 0–3 °C 72 h; freeze −18 °C 3 months.

=== Porrusalda · Basque Leek & Potato Soup · Salt Cod

tag Soups / Vegetables
## SOUP
Leeks | 1500
Potatoes | 1200
Carrot | 300
Garlic | 20
Olive oil | 120
Salt cod, desalted | 300
Water | 2500
Salt | 12
Yield | 4000
1. Simmer desalted cod in the water 5 min; lift out and flake; keep the water.

=== Patatas a la Riojana · Rioja Potato & Chorizo Stew · Choricero Pepper

Choricero peppers, dried | 30
Olive oil | 100
Sweet paprika | 10
Hot paprika | 3
Bay leaves | 2
Water | 2200
Salt | 20
Yield | 4800
1. Soak choriceros in hot water 1 h; scrape the pulp.
2. Sweat onion, pepper and garlic in oil 15 min; add chorizo in 2 cm slices 5 min; paprika and pepper pulp.

=== Allioli Tradicional · Hand-Pounded Garlic & Olive Oil Emulsion

## ALLIOLI
: True allioli has no egg · if it splits, start again with a little garlic and add the broken sauce drop by drop
Garlic | 60
Salt | 5
Olive oil, mild | 400
Lemon juice | 5
Yield | 450
1. Pound peeled garlic (germ removed) with salt in a mortar to a fine paste.
2. Add oil drop by drop, pounding in one direction, until a thick, white emulsion forms (15–20 min). Finish with lemon.
st 0–4 °C 48 h in a covered container. Raw-garlic sauce: label with date.

=== Tocino de Cielo · Egg-Yolk & Syrup Caramel Custard
at Spanish & Tapas / Postres (Desserts) · N 16 · pg 70 · portions
tag Desserts
## CARAMEL
Sugar | 200
Water | 50
1. Cook to a deep amber caramel; pour into a 30 × 20 cm tin.
## CUSTARD
Sugar | 500
Water | 250
Lemon peel | 5
Egg yolks | 480
Eggs | 120
Yield | 1200
1. Boil sugar, water and lemon peel to a thread syrup (107 °C); cool to 60 °C; remove peel.
2. Mix yolks and eggs gently (no foam); pour in the syrup while stirring; pass through a fine sieve.
3. Pour over the set caramel; cover with foil; bake in a bain-marie at 150 °C 40–45 min until set (core ≥ 75 °C).
4. Cool; chill ≤ 5 °C overnight. Turn out; cut 16 squares.
st 0–4 °C 4 days.

=== Leche Merengada · Iced Cinnamon & Lemon Milk Meringue

tag Desserts
## MILK
Milk | 1500
Sugar | 250
Cinnamon sticks | 6
Lemon peel | 8
1. Bring milk, sugar, cinnamon and peel to a simmer; infuse off the heat 30 min; strain; cool to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min.
## MERINGUE
Egg whites, pasteurised | 240
Sugar | 60
Cinnamon | 3
Yield | 1900
1. Whip pasteurised whites with sugar to soft peaks; fold into the cold milk.
2. Freeze in a shallow tray, forking every 30 min, until slushy (about 3 h); or churn briefly.
