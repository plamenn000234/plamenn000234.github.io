## PLUMS
: Classic side for Kaiserschmarrn, Germknödel and Topfenknödel; use ripe late-season Zwetschgen
Plums, stoned | 1880
Sugar | 200
Water | 100
Cinnamon sticks | 2
Cloves | 3
Lemon zest | 3
Dark rum | 40
Yield | 1600
1. Halve and stone plums. Caramelise sugar lightly; add water, cinnamon, cloves and zest.
2. Add plums; cook covered over low heat 15–20 min, stirring rarely, until soft but holding shape. Add rum.
st Hot-filled into sterilised jars and inverted: 3 months; opened 0–3 °C 10 days.
