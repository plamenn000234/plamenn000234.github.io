=== Tavče Gravče · Macedonian Baked Beans in Clay · Dried Red Pepper, Mint
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bosnia, Montenegro & N. Macedonia · N 12 · pg 300 · bowls
## BEANS
: Soak 12 h · large white beans
Dried white beans | 1000
Water | 4000
Bay leaves | 2
1. Soak beans 12 h; drain. Cover with fresh water, boil 5 min, discard water.
2. Cover with 4 L fresh water, add bay; simmer 1 h 15 min until just tender, not broken. Keep 1.2 L liquid.
## ZAPRŠKA
Onion | 500
Sunflower oil | 150
Dried red peppers | 30
Wheat flour | 30
Paprika, sweet | 20
Chilli flakes | 3
Mint, dried | 4
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 18
1. Sweat sliced onion in oil 12 min; add crushed dried peppers 1 min; stir in flour 1 min, then paprika and chilli off the heat.
## BAKE
Yield | 3600
1. Combine drained beans, zaprška, mint, pepper, salt and enough cooking liquid to just cover.
2. Bake in a clay dish (or GN 1/1) at 200 °C uncovered 40–50 min until a crust forms and the liquid is thick. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 300 g in a hot clay bowl; pickled peppers and bread on the side. Optional: smoked sausage on top (adjust data).
st Hot hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat 180 °C to ≥ 75 °C, adding water if dry.

=== Selsko Meso · Macedonian Village Pork & Mushroom Pot · Peppers, White Wine
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bosnia, Montenegro & N. Macedonia · N 10 · pg 350 · pots
## MEAT
Pork neck, boneless | 1800
Sunflower oil | 60
Salt | 18
Black pepper | 4
1. Cut pork into 2.5 cm cubes; season; sear in batches in a very hot pan; set aside.
## SAUCE
Onion | 500
Button mushrooms | 800
Red peppers | 400
Smoked sausage | 300
Garlic | 30
Paprika, sweet | 15
Tomato paste | 60
Dry white wine | 300
Water | 400
Bay leaves | 2
Parsley | 20
Yield | 3500
1. Sweat onion 8 min; add peppers and sliced sausage 5 min; add quartered mushrooms, cook until their water evaporates.
2. Add garlic, paprika and tomato paste 1 min; deglaze with wine, reduce by half.

## SERVICE
1. 350 g in an individual clay pot; bread on the side.
st Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat in pots 200 °C to ≥ 75 °C. Hot hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h.

=== Raštan sa Suvim Mesom · Montenegrin Collard Greens · Smoked Pork, Potato
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bosnia, Montenegro & N. Macedonia · N 10 · pg 380 · bowls
## STEW
Collard greens | 2000
Smoked pork ribs | 1000
Smoked bacon | 300
Potatoes | 1000
Onion | 300
Garlic | 30
Olive oil | 80
Water | 2000
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 10
Yield | 3800
1. Simmer smoked ribs in water 1 h; lift out, cut into pieces; keep the broth.
2. Strip collard leaves from stalks; cut into 3 cm ribbons; wash well.

## SERVICE
