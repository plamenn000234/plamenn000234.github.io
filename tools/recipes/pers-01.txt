Eggs | 100

Spinach | 300
Dill | 60
Coriander, fresh | 60

Salt | 18
## FINISH
Butter | 50
Dried mint | 8
Yield | 4500
1. Whisk yoghurt, water, flour and egg until smooth; heat gently, stirring constantly in one direction, until it boils (15 min) and stabilises.
2. Add washed rice; simmer 15 min.

## FINISH
