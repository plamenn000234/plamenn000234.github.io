=== Bratkartoffeln · Pan-Fried Potatoes · Bacon, Onion & Marjoram

Smoked streaky bacon, diced | 300
Onion | 400

Dried marjoram | 2
Salt | 16
Black pepper | 2
Chives | 15
Yield | 2400
fry Clarified butter
1. Boil potatoes in their skins until just tender (core 90 °C); cool overnight at 0–3 °C, peel and slice 5 mm.
2. Render the bacon in a wide pan; add clarified butter and potatoes in one layer; do not turn for 6 min until a crust forms.
3. Turn, add sliced onion; fry 8 min more until golden. Season with salt, pepper and marjoram; finish with chives.

=== Spargel mit Sauce Hollandaise · White Asparagus · Hollandaise, New Potatoes, Ham

## ASPARAGUS
White asparagus, thick | 2500
Water | 5000
Salt | 50
Sugar | 30
Butter | 50
Lemon | 60
1. Peel asparagus from below the tip; cut off 1–2 cm of woody end. Keep peels and ends.
2. Simmer peels and ends in water with salt, sugar, butter and lemon 15 min; strain.
3. Cook asparagus in the stock 10–14 min until just tender at the base; hold in the stock at ≥ 63 °C up to 20 min.
## HOLLANDAISE
Egg yolks | 160
White wine | 60
Water | 40
Butter, clarified | 500
Lemon juice | 30
Salt | 4
Cayenne pepper | 0.5
1. Whisk yolks with wine and water over a bain-marie to a thick ribbon (70 °C).
2. Off the heat whisk in warm clarified butter (55 °C) in a thin stream; season with lemon, salt and cayenne.
3. Hold at 60–63 °C maximum 2 h; discard after service.
## GARNISH
New potatoes | 1500
Cooked ham, thinly sliced | 600
Parsley | 15
Yield | 5000
1. Boil potatoes in salted water; toss with parsley.
2. Plate asparagus with potatoes, ham and hollandaise on the side.
st Peeled raw asparagus wrapped in damp cloth 0–3 °C 48 h; hollandaise not stored.
