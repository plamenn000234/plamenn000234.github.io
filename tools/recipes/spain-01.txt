=== Ensalada Murciana · Murcian Tomato Salad · Tuna, Egg, Black Olives
at Spanish & Tapas / Tapas Frías (Cold Tapas) · N 12 · pg 150 · plates
tag Salads / Fish
## SALAD
: Best with ripe summer tomatoes or canned whole peeled tomatoes in winter (the traditional winter version)
Tomatoes, ripe | 1200
Spring onions | 120
Tuna in olive oil, drained | 300
Eggs | 360
Black olives, pitted | 120
Olive oil, extra virgin | 120
Sherry vinegar | 20
Salt | 10
Oregano, dried | 2
Yield | 1800
1. Boil eggs 10 min from boiling water, cool in iced water, peel and quarter.
2. Peel tomatoes (cross-cut, 10 s in boiling water, iced water); cut into 2 cm chunks, keep juices.
3. Toss tomatoes with sliced spring onion, oil, vinegar, salt and oregano; rest 15 min at 0–4 °C.
4. Plate 150 g; top with flaked tuna, egg quarters and olives.
st Dressed tomatoes 0–4 °C 12 h. Eggs peeled 0–4 °C 48 h. Assemble to order.

## PORRA
: Thicker than salmorejo · a spoon must stand in it

Green pepper | 100

Garlic | 15
Olive oil, extra virgin | 250
Sherry vinegar | 40
Salt | 16
Yield | 2200
1. Blend tomatoes and pepper until smooth; pass through a fine sieve.
2. Soak torn bread in the tomato liquid 20 min; add garlic, salt and vinegar; blend 2 min.

2. Serve porra in chilled bowls topped with egg, ham and tuna; a drizzle of oil.
st Porra 0–4 °C 48 h, covered. Garnish 0–4 °C 48 h.

Eggs | 180

=== Codornices en Escabeche · Pickled Quail · Thyme, Bay, Sherry Vinegar
at Spanish & Tapas / Tapas Frías (Cold Tapas) · N 12 · pg 220 · portions (1 quail)
tag Starters & canapés / Game
## QUAIL
: Make 2–5 days ahead; flavour improves
Quail, whole | 2160
Salt | 15
Olive oil | 400
Garlic | 60
Onion | 300
Carrot | 200
Bay leaves | 4
Thyme | 6
Black peppercorns | 4
White wine | 300
Sherry vinegar | 250
Water | 200
Sweet paprika | 5
Yield | 3200
1. Truss quail; salt 30 min. Brown in 100 g oil 6–8 min; set aside.
2. In the remaining oil sweat whole garlic, sliced onion and carrot 10 min; add herbs and peppercorns.
3. Add wine, vinegar, water and paprika; bring to a boil; return quail; simmer covered 20 min (core ≥ 75 °C).
4. Cool in the liquid; chill to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min. Keep covered by the escabeche.
## SERVICE
1. Serve one quail per portion at room temperature with some of the vegetables and liquor.
st 0–4 °C 7 days in the escabeche, fully covered.

=== Callos a la Madrileña · Madrid Tripe Stew · Chorizo, Morcilla, Paprika
at Spanish & Tapas / Tapas Calientes (Hot Tapas) · N 14 · pg 250 · cazuelas
tag Starters & canapés / Beef & veal
## TRIPE
: Better the next day · gelatinous, sticky sauce
Beef tripe, cleaned | 2000
Pig's trotters | 1000
Beef cheek | 600
Bay leaves | 3
Onion | 200
Black peppercorns | 4
Salt | 20
Water | 5000
1. Blanch tripe and trotters 10 min; drain, rinse. Cut tripe into 4 cm squares.
2. Cover tripe, trotters and cheek with fresh water; add bay, onion, pepper and salt; simmer 3–3.5 h until very tender.
3. Bone the trotters; cut skin and cheek into pieces. Keep 1.5 l of the stock.
## SOFRITO

Onion | 300
Garlic | 30
Chorizo, cooking | 400
Morcilla | 300

Sweet paprika | 20

Wheat flour | 20

Yield | 4000

3. Rest overnight at 0–4 °C (cool to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min); reheat to ≥ 75 °C.
st 0–3 °C 72 h. Freeze −18 °C 2 months.
