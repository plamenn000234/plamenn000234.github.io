=== Shkembe Chorba · Bulgarian Tripe Soup · Garlic-Vinegar Dressing, Chilli
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 350 · bowls
## TRIPE
: Blanched and cleaned honeycomb tripe · cooked weight ≈ 1.1 kg
Beef tripe, cleaned | 1500
Water | 5000
Bay leaves | 2
Salt | 15
Yield | 1100
1. Rinse the tripe under cold running water 10 min; blanch from cold water, boil 5 min, drain and rinse (removes smell and grey scum).
2. Cover with 5 L fresh water, add bay and salt; simmer covered 2 h 30 min until a knife slides in easily.
3. Lift out the tripe; strain and keep 2.5 L of the cooking liquor.
4. Cut the tripe into 1 × 4 cm strips. Critical point: if not used at once, chill to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min.
## SOUP
: GN 1/1 × 100 mm or 6 L pot · ≈ 3.5 kg
Tripe, cooked (above) | 1100
Tripe cooking liquor (above) | 2500
Butter | 80
Sunflower oil | 40
Paprika, sweet | 15
Milk 3.6% | 500
Black pepper | 3
Salt | 12
Yield | 3500
1. Melt butter with oil at 90 °C (do not brown); take off the heat, stir in paprika 20 s so it colours without burning.
2. Add the tripe and liquor; bring to a simmer 10 min.
3. Add milk; heat to 85–90 °C without boiling hard (milk would split). Season.
4. Hold for service at ≥ 63 °C, max 2 h.
## DRESSING (ON THE SIDE)
: 10 portions · serve in a cruet
Garlic | 60
White wine vinegar | 200
Water | 50
Salt | 3
1. Crush garlic with salt; stir in vinegar and water. Make on the day; keep at 0–4 °C.
## SERVICE
1. Ladle 350 g into a hot bowl; offer the garlic-vinegar dressing and dried chilli flakes on the side.
st Soup 0–3 °C 48 h (cook-chill: 60 → 10 °C within 2 h); reheat once to ≥ 75 °C core. Cooked tripe 0–3 °C 72 h. Dressing 0–4 °C 24 h.

=== Kapama Banska · Bansko Layered Meat & Sauerkraut Pot · Rice, Blood Sausage
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 420 · plates
## MEAT
: Bone-in · browned in batches
Pork neck, boneless | 1200
Chicken legs, bone-in | 1200
Rabbit, jointed | 800
Sunflower oil | 80
Salt | 18
Black pepper | 4
Paprika, sweet | 10
1. Cut pork into 80 g pieces; season all meat with salt, pepper and paprika.
2. Brown in a 220 °C pan in small batches (crowding steams the meat); set aside.
## RICE & CABBAGE
: Sauerkraut rinsed once if very sour
Sauerkraut, shredded | 1500
Long-grain rice | 300
Onion | 300
Kaiserfleisch, diced | 200
Blood sausage (karvavitsa) | 300
Allspice | 3
Bay leaves | 3
Savory, dried | 5
Water | 1200
1. Sweat onion and kaiserfleisch 8 min; stir in washed rice and savory 2 min.
2. In an oiled clay pot or deep GN 1/1, layer: sauerkraut, meat, rice mixture, sausage pieces, sauerkraut; finish with a sauerkraut layer.
3. Add bay, allspice and water to just below the top layer.
## BAKE
: Covered · 160 °C
Yield | 4200
1. Seal with foil (traditionally a dough lid); bake 160 °C 3 h 30 min.
2. Check: rice cooked, meat falls from the bone, core ≥ 75 °C. Rest 15 min before portioning.
## SERVICE
1. Portion 420 g so each plate gets pork, chicken, rabbit and sausage; serve in the pot or on a hot plate with rye bread.
st Hot hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill: 0–3 °C 72 h; reheat covered 160 °C to ≥ 75 °C core.

=== Kyopoolu · Roasted Aubergine & Pepper Spread · Garlic, Walnuts, Parsley
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 12 · pg 100 · portions
tag Starters & canapés / Vegetables
## ROAST
: Charcoal, gas flame or 250 °C oven · roasted weight ≈ 1.4 kg
Aubergines | 1800
Red peppers (kapia) | 800
1. Char aubergines and peppers on all sides until the skin is black and the flesh collapses (15–20 min).
2. Peppers: steam in a covered bowl 10 min, peel and seed. Aubergines: split, scoop the flesh, drain in a sieve 30 min (bitter juice out).
## SPREAD
: Hand-chopped, not blended
Roasted aubergine flesh (above) | 1000
Roasted peppers (above) | 400
Garlic | 40
Sunflower oil | 120
Red wine vinegar | 40
Walnuts, toasted | 80
Parsley | 40
Salt | 12
Yield | 1200
1. Chop aubergine and peppers with a knife to a coarse texture.
2. Mix with crushed garlic, oil, vinegar and salt; fold in chopped walnuts and parsley.
3. Chill to ≤ 5 °C within 90 min; rest 2 h for the flavours to set.
## SERVICE
1. 100 g per portion, spooned and swirled, drizzle of oil, a few walnut halves; serve with warm pitka or grilled bread.
st 0–4 °C 72 h in a covered container, surface covered with oil. Do not freeze.

=== Teleshka Chorba · Veal & Root Vegetable Soup · Yoghurt-Egg Liaison, Lovage
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 12 · pg 350 · bowls
## VEAL STOCK & MEAT
: Simmered, never boiled · strained stock ≈ 3 L
Veal shoulder, boneless | 1000
Veal bones | 1000
Water | 4500
Onion | 150
Bay leaves | 2
Black peppercorns | 3
Salt | 15
1. Blanch bones from cold water 5 min, rinse. Cover bones and meat with 4.5 L cold water; bring slowly to 90 °C, skim.
2. Add onion, bay and peppercorns; simmer 1 h 45 min until the meat is tender.
3. Lift out the meat, cut into 1.5 cm dice; strain stock through a fine chinois (≈ 3 L).
## SOUP
: 8 L pot · ≈ 4.2 kg before liaison
Veal stock (above) | 3000
Veal, cooked and diced (above) | 750
Carrots | 250
Celeriac | 150
Potatoes | 400
Red peppers | 150
Onion | 150
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 250
Sunflower oil | 40
Paprika, sweet | 8
Salt | 10
1. Cut vegetables into 1 cm dice. Sweat onion, carrot and celeriac in oil 6 min; add paprika 20 s off the heat.
2. Add stock, potatoes and peppers; simmer 15 min. Add tomatoes and veal; simmer 10 min.
## LIAISON (ZASTROIKA)
: Tempered, added off the boil
Yoghurt 3.6% | 300
Eggs | 150
Wheat flour | 30
Lovage | 10
Parsley | 20
Yield | 4200
1. Whisk yoghurt, eggs and flour smooth. Temper with 500 ml hot soup, whisking.
2. Off the heat, stir the liaison into the soup; return to 80–85 °C, stirring, 2 min. Do not boil (eggs curdle).
3. Finish with chopped lovage and parsley. Core temperature ≥ 75 °C before holding.
## SERVICE
1. 350 g in a hot bowl, a few drops of red wine vinegar optional, chilli on the side.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 48 h; reheat gently to ≥ 75 °C, stirring, once only (liaison).

=== Leshta Chorba · Brown Lentil Soup · Savory, Mint, Paprika Oil
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 12 · pg 330 · bowls
tag Soups / Legumes
## SOUP
: Lenten · vegan · 8 L pot
Brown lentils | 500
Onion | 250
Carrots | 200
Garlic | 25
Tomatoes, peeled, can | 300
Sunflower oil | 80
Paprika, sweet | 10
Savory, dried | 4
Mint, dried | 3
Bay leaves | 2
Water | 3200
Salt | 20
Red wine vinegar | 20
Yield | 4000
1. Pick and rinse lentils; cover with cold water and bring to a boil, drain (cleaner taste).
2. Sweat onion and carrot in half the oil 8 min; add garlic 1 min, paprika 20 s off the heat.
3. Add lentils, water and bay; simmer 25 min. Add tomatoes and savory; simmer 10 min until lentils are soft but whole.
4. Season with salt, mint and vinegar at the end (salt early toughens skins).
## PAPRIKA OIL
Sunflower oil | 40
Paprika, smoked | 4

## SERVICE
1. 330 g per bowl, paprika oil and chopped parsley; serve with rye bread.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 3 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; thicken on reheat, so add hot water to correct; reheat to ≥ 75 °C.

=== Supa ot Kopriva · Spring Nettle Soup · Rice, Soft Egg, Yoghurt
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 330 · bowls
tag Soups / Vegetables
## SOUP
: Young nettle tops only (March–May) · wear gloves
Nettles, young tops | 800
Spinach | 300
Onion | 150
Spring onions | 100
Long-grain rice | 80
Butter | 60
Wheat flour | 30
Vegetable stock | 2500
Salt | 15
Black pepper | 2
Yield | 3200
1. Wash nettles three times; blanch 1 min in boiling salted water, refresh in iced water, squeeze and chop finely (blanching removes the sting and keeps the green colour).
2. Sweat onion and spring onion in butter 5 min; add flour 1 min; whisk in hot stock.
3. Add rice; simmer 15 min. Add nettles and spinach; simmer 3 min only.
4. Blend one third and return for body; season.
## GARNISH
Eggs (soft-boiled) | 300
Yoghurt 3.6% | 300
1. Boil eggs 6 min 30 s from boiling; chill in iced water, peel. Hold at 0–4 °C.
## SERVICE
1. 330 g soup, half a soft egg, 30 g yoghurt, a twist of black pepper.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 90 min (colour fades). Chill 0–3 °C 24 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C once. Eggs 0–4 °C 48 h.

=== Gabena Supa · Wild Mushroom & Potato Soup · Sour Cream, Dill
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 330 · bowls
## SOUP
: Fresh forest mushrooms or champignons + dried porcini
Mushrooms, mixed forest | 900
Dried porcini | 30
Potatoes | 500
Onion | 200
Carrots | 150
Butter | 80
Sunflower oil | 30
Garlic | 15
Vegetable stock | 2200
Thyme | 5
Salt | 15
Black pepper | 3
Yield | 3200
1. Soak porcini in 400 ml warm water 30 min; chop; strain the soaking liquor through a coffee filter and add it to the stock.
2. Slice fresh mushrooms; sauté in hot oil in batches until golden (water must evaporate first).
3. Sweat onion and carrot in butter 6 min; add garlic and thyme 1 min.
4. Add stock, porcini and potatoes (1 cm dice); simmer 15 min; add sautéed mushrooms 5 min. Season.
## FINISH
Sour cream 20% | 250
Dill | 25
1. Temper sour cream with a ladle of soup; stir in off the boil; add dill.
## SERVICE
1. 330 g per bowl with a spoon of sour cream and dill.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h. Cook-chill 0–3 °C 48 h; reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Mushroom soups: never hold warm overnight.

=== Tas Kebap · Braised Beef in Onion & Wine Sauce · Mashed Potatoes
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 380 · plates
## BEEF
: Braising pan · 2.5 h
Beef chuck, boneless | 1800
Sunflower oil | 80
Onion | 800
Wheat flour | 40
Tomato paste | 40
Red wine | 300
Beef stock | 900
Paprika, sweet | 10
Bay leaves | 2
Black pepper | 3
Allspice | 2
Salt | 20
Yield | 2300
1. Cut beef into 3 cm cubes, pat dry; brown in hot oil in batches; remove.
2. Sweat finely chopped onion 15 min until soft and sweet; dust with flour, add paprika and tomato paste, cook 2 min.
3. Deglaze with wine, reduce by half; add stock, spices and beef. Cover and braise at 150 °C 2 h – 2 h 30 min until tender.
4. Correct sauce to a coating consistency; season. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## MASH
Potatoes | 1800
Butter | 120
Milk 3.6% | 300
Salt | 15
Yield | 1800
1. Steam or boil potatoes; dry 2 min; pass through a ricer.
2. Beat in hot milk and butter; season. Hold ≥ 63 °C covered.
## SERVICE
1. 180 g mash, 200 g beef with sauce, chopped parsley.
st Beef cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h or −18 °C 2 months; reheat to ≥ 75 °C. Mash made fresh, hold ≥ 63 °C max 2 h.

=== Svinsko s Kiselo Zele · Oven-Braised Pork Shoulder with Sauerkraut · Paprika, Bay
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 400 · plates
## PORK
Pork shoulder, boneless | 2000
Sunflower oil | 60
Salt | 14
Black pepper | 3
1. Cut pork into 100 g pieces; season and brown on all sides in a 220 °C rondeau.
## SAUERKRAUT
: Taste first; rinse once if very sour
Sauerkraut, shredded | 2000
Onion | 300
Paprika, sweet | 12
Chilli flakes | 2
Bay leaves | 3
Black peppercorns | 3
Sauerkraut brine | 300
Water | 300
Yield | 4000

=== Patitsa s Kiselo Zele · Roast Duck Legs on Sauerkraut · Onion, Apple
at Adriatic & Balkan / Bulgaria · N 10 · pg 380 · plates
## DUCK
: Salted the day before
Duck legs, bone-in | 2500
Salt | 25
Black pepper | 4
Garlic | 20
1. Season legs with salt, pepper and crushed garlic; refrigerate uncovered 12 h at 0–4 °C (dries the skin).
2. Roast skin-up on a rack 150 °C 1 h 30 min; raise to 200 °C 15 min for crisp skin. Keep the fat.
## SAUERKRAUT
Sauerkraut, shredded | 1600
Onion | 300
Apples | 300
Duck fat | 60
Paprika, sweet | 8
Bay leaves | 2
Water | 300
1. Sweat onion in duck fat; add paprika off the heat; mix with sauerkraut, diced apple, bay and water.
2. Spread in a GN 1/1; set the half-roasted legs on top for the last 45 min so the fat drips into the cabbage. Core ≥ 75 °C.
## SERVICE
1. One leg on 200 g sauerkraut.
st Hold ≥ 63 °C max 90 min (skin softens). Cook-chill 0–3 °C 72 h; re-crisp skin-up 200 °C 12 min to ≥ 75 °C.
