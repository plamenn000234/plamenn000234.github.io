Chefsheet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Chefsheet is a recipe and kitchen-training app. It is built to work without an account and does not collect personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Chefsheet has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data.

What stays on your device

To make the app useful, it saves the following only in your device's browser storage:

  • Favourite recipes and recently viewed recipes
  • Your order lists and saved orders
  • Lesson progress, test results and the name you enter for a certificate
  • Recipes saved for offline use

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Files you create

PDF and CSV files (recipes, order lists, certificates) are created on your device and saved where you choose. When you tap Share, the text goes only to the app you pick.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and a PDF library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when the files are downloaded. They do not receive any of your app data. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Children

Chefsheet is meant for adults working in kitchens. Some recipes contain alcohol. The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Recipe information

Allergen, diet and nutrition information is derived from the listed ingredients and is an estimate. Always check the labels of the products you use.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
